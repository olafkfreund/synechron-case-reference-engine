# Spec: outputs in the Synechron house style

## Design

The rules come from `synechron-pptx-v2/SKILL.md`, which is authoritative.
The artwork comes from the official June 2026 templates in `synechron-pptx`.
Rendering both templates shows:

- **Light template:** content slides on light grey `#F4F4F4`, title top
  left, small wordmark bottom right. Its cover ("Title Slide") and closing
  ("Thank You ") put the yellow device on **grey**.
- **Dark template:** the cover and "Thank You " put the yellow device on
  Synechron blue `#002535`, with a white wordmark top left. That is v2's
  cover option 2, which v2 makes mandatory for the closing slide.

So decks use **Light content slides with the blue cover and closing from
the Dark design.** v2 wins where the two templates differ.

### 1. Fonts: Aptos installed in the image

- **`Dockerfile` downloads and installs Aptos.** A new step downloads the
  Microsoft Aptos Fonts 4.40 zip, checks its pinned SHA-256
  (`6528fd12…b025`) and fails the build if it doesn't match. It extracts
  `Aptos`, `Aptos-Light`, `Aptos-SemiBold`, `Aptos-Bold`, `Aptos-Italic`
  and `Aptos-Mono` to `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/aptos/`, then runs
  `fc-cache`. The fonts are never committed (EULA, see intent).
- **CI gets them the same way:** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` already builds
  this image.
- **`app/archdiagram.py` measures text with the installed Aptos** instead
  of vendored IBM Plex. `FONTS` points at the system directory, and the
  weights map to Aptos files.
- **IBM Plex leaves the repo.** `brand/fonts/` and its OFL licence are
  removed, since nothing uses them any more. `tests/test_fonts.py` now
  checks that the Aptos weights are installed and measurable.
- **Technical notes use `Aptos Mono`** instead of `Consolas`, which isn't
  in the container (`render.py:321`).

### 2. PowerPoint: `brand/master.pptx` from the official Light template

A new `scripts/make_brand_master.py <light.potx> <out.pptx>` replaces
`make_reference_master.py`. It:

1. Opens the `.potx` as a presentation (content-type fix in memory) and
   deletes all 24 sample slides.
2. **Keeps the Light master and theme** (Aptos theme fonts, footer,
   wordmark, slide number).
3. **Cover and closing.** Keeps "Title Slide" as **"Cover"** and
   "Thank You " as **"Closing"**, recoloured to cover option 2:
   - solid `#002535` background
   - the white wordmark (the template's own white SVG and its PNG fallback)
   - the yellow device left as it is, never redrawn

   The closing slide keeps its "Thank you" text in the clear left area.
   The contact block and CTA pills are removed, because the engine has no
   contact to put there.
4. **Content layouts.** Adds "Reference case", "Industry context" and
   "Diagram" as copies of "One column", so they inherit its background,
   wordmark, footer and number. Each has exactly the contract placeholders
   (`render.py:30`, `:364`, `:368`), with unique idx values:
   - **Title:** the template's title style.
   - **Client:** `#0A5A78`.
   - **Section headings** (Challenge, Solution, Outcomes, Technology):
     static text in Aptos Bold, with a short yellow `#FBF321` keyline as
     the only accent.
   - **Body:** Aptos 11–12 pt, black.
   - **Rounded containers:** 6–10 px radius on any box.
   - **Logo clear space:** no placeholder sits in the top-right
     50 × 25 mm box, so the client logo
     (`render.py:459`) never collides with the template's own artwork.
5. **Shrinks the file.** Removes every other layout and all media they no
   longer reference, so the committed master stays small (target under
   1.5 MB; the source is 2.1 MB).

**`render.to_pptx` changes:**
- adds a **Cover** slide first, titled "Reference cases" with the month and
  year
- adds a **Closing** slide last
- leaves everything in between unchanged

The deck is cover → cases (+ diagrams) → industry context → closing.

The source `.potx` isn't committed. The script runs once, by a maintainer,
and its output `brand/master.pptx` is. The wordmark files are committed as
`brand/wordmark-{white,black}.svg` and `.png`, at 2000 px wide with a
transparent background. The PNGs are the template's own fallbacks, or are
rasterised once with LibreOffice.

### 3. Word: the three templates in the brand

`scripts/make_reference_template.py` keeps generating `reference.docx`,
`case_study.docx` and `brief.docx`. The docxtpl tags and contexts don't
change. Only the styling does:

- **Styles:**
  - Normal: Aptos 10.5 pt, black.
  - Title and Heading 1: Aptos Light or Bold, `#002535`.
  - Heading 2: Aptos Bold, `#002535`, with a thin yellow keyline under
    the case title only.
  - Caption, disclaimer and notes: `#808285`.
  - List Bullet: Aptos.
  - Technical notes: Aptos Mono.
- **Header:** the black wordmark top left, at its clear space (one "S"
  height). No "DRAFT TEMPLATE" marker.
- **Footer:** "Synechron · Confidential" and a PAGE field, in `#808285`.
- **Case study first page:** a full-width `#002535` band with the white
  wordmark and the case title in white, plus the client logo cell kept on
  the right, when allowed.
- **Callouts** (summary, industry disclaimer): shaded `#F4F4F4`, borderless.
  Word tables can't have rounded corners, so this is the accepted
  exception (see Risks).
- **Reference document:** the one-case-per-page structure stays.

### 4. PDF

There's no separate styling: `to_pdf` converts the DOCX or PPTX with
LibreOffice. With Aptos installed, PDFs use Aptos, and `pdffonts` must list
it as embedded. Embedding is allowed (fsType `0x8`).

### 5. Diagrams

The colours in `app/archdiagram.py` (`:636-649`) move onto the palette:

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| ink | `#002535` |
| ground | `#F4F4F4` |
| accent | `#0A5A78` |
| border and muted | `#808285` |
| on dark | `#FFFFFF` |

Node kinds and edge modes are no longer colour-coded with off-palette hues.
They use the two blues, black and grey, and their labels and shapes tell
them apart. Yellow `#FBF321` is used only for one highlight style, as an
accent.

The text font is Aptos, and box corners stay rounded.

### 6. Companion skills, outside this repo

- **`synechron-docx/` and `synechron-pdf/`** are written next to
  `synechron-pptx-v2/` in the skills folder. Each has a `SKILL.md` and
  `plugin.json` in the same structure as v2.
- **Content:** the v2 colour, logo, yellow-accent and typography rules,
  adapted to Word and PDF: styles, header and footer, the cover band,
  callouts, and the no-rounded-tables exception.
- **Assets:** they point to the official templates for the wordmark.
- **Distribution:** they ship no fonts; Aptos comes from Microsoft's
  download, per the EULA.

## Alternatives rejected

- **The Light template's own cover and Thank You.** Grey breaks v2's
  mandatory closing rule.
- **The whole Dark template.** The requester chose Light, which prints
  better and matches the documents.
- **The v2 cover JPGs as full-bleed backgrounds.** At 992×558 they blur
  in PDF and print.
- **Committing the Aptos files.** The EULA gives no distribution rights.
- **A fallback open font for PDF.** The requester chose Aptos everywhere.
- **Rewriting `to_pptx` to drive the template's own 19 layouts.** It is
  more code, and loses the tested named-placeholder contract.

## Risks

- **Microsoft moves or changes the download.** The build fails on the hash
  check, which is loud rather than silent. Fix by updating the URL and
  hash.
- **LibreOffice renders the template's SVG artwork differently from
  PowerPoint.** Mitigation: the PNG fallbacks stay in the package, and the
  covers are checked visually in PDF.
- **Word has no rounded tables.** Callouts are shaded and borderless.
  This is an accepted exception to "rounded containers", stated in the
  `synechron-docx` skill.
- **Dense case text on one slide.** The renderer can't enforce "one
  message per slide". The existing field caps (300 characters for
  Challenge and Solution, 5 outcomes) stay. Overflow is checked visually on
  the longest demo case.
- **Tests that pin the placeholders will change:**
  - the "DRAFT TEMPLATE" markers (`tests/test_render.py:46`, `:357`)
  - the slide counts (cover and closing add 2)
  - the IBM Plex font test
  - the client-logo picture counts, if the wordmark ever lands on a slide
    instead of the layout

  They are updated to assert the brand instead.
- **The image grows** by about 6 MB of fonts. The repo shrinks: IBM Plex
  is removed, and the placeholder master is replaced.
