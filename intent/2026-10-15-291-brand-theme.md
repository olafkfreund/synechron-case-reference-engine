# Intent: the portal uses the Synechron business web template's brand

## Problem

There is now an internal business web template for Synechron web pages:
`synechron-web-template`, a Next.js, React, Tailwind and shadcn starter.
The portal doesn't follow it:

- **Accent colour:** amber (`#FDC700`) with brown text, where the brand
  uses Core Blue (`#002535`) and Light Blue (`#26A9E0`).
- **Dark mode:** a neutral black, where the brand uses navy.
- **Text and corners:** body text is 15 px, below the brand's 16 px
  minimum, and corners are rounder than the brand's 8 px.
- **Logo:** the header shows the name "Reference Engine" without the
  Synechron logo.

## Proposed outcome

**The portal looks like a Synechron page, in light and dark mode:**

- **Light:** page `#F4F4F4`, cards white, text `#001422`.
  - **Accent and active tab:** Core Blue `#002535` with white text.
  - **Links and focus:** use the brand blues.
- **Dark ("Night Navy"):** page `#000C14`, cards `#001422`, raised
  surfaces `#002535`, and Light Blue for links and focus.
- **Yellow `#FBF321`** appears only as a small decorative mark, never as a
  button and never with text on it in light mode.
- **Body text** is 16 px, and controls and cards have 8 px corners.
- **Logo:** the header shows the Synechron logo next to the product name.
  - **Colour:** navy in light mode, white in dark mode.
  - **Size:** at least 200 px wide. On narrow screens the brandmark is
    used instead, as the template's rules say.

**Nothing else changes:**

- **No change to the page layouts or what each page does.** The theme
  toggle keeps working as today.
- **The template's code is not used.** The portal stays server-rendered
  Jinja with no React, Tailwind or new JavaScript. Only the brand values
  (colours, sizes, logo) are carried over.

## Affected users and systems

- **Code:**
  - `app/static/portal.css`: the tokens block and its three themes, plus a
    few rules that hard-code sizes
  - `app/templates/base.html`: the header
  - one or two new SVG files under `app/static/`
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_ui.py` checks:
    - hex-only colours, kept inside the tokens block
    - that the two dark blocks match
    - the contrast ratio of each colour pair
    - the theme-toggle markup
  - `tests/test_assets.py` checks the font faces and touch targets.
- **People:** everyone who uses the portal. Dense tables and review
  screens get slightly larger with 16 px text.

## Constraints

- **Contrast:** every colour pair still passes the contrast test. That
  includes the ok, warn and bad badges and the danger button on the navy
  backgrounds.
- **Colour format:** the template defines its colours in OKLCH, so they
  are converted to 6-digit hex. Colours stay inside the tokens block.
- **Fonts:** stay self-hosted. Montserrat is already in the repo under the
  OFL. No Google Fonts or other external requests, so the security headers
  don't change. Aptos font files are never committed (EULA).
- **Template files:** the logo SVG has a `<style>` block, which the
  template test refuses. The repo gets a cleaned copy that draws in
  `currentColor`. The template's brand PDF and guideline documents are
  not copied.
- **Accessibility:** the logo has a text alternative and the product name
  stays as text. Focus rings stay visible in both themes.

## Open questions

None. Decided on 2026-10-15, when the requester took the recommended
answers:

1. **Logo:** the Synechron logo is shipped in this internal repo as a
   cleaned SVG, at 200 px or wider in the header, and the brandmark is used
   on narrow screens.
2. **Active tab:** the active nav tab is Core Blue, not yellow.
3. **Body text:** 16 px, the brand minimum, including the dense tables.
4. **Dark mode:** the brand's Night Navy, not the current neutral dark.
