# Intent: Client logos in the registry and in outputs

## Problem

The client registry (`/admin/clients`) has a **Logo allowed** flag, but
nothing behind it:
- an admin has nowhere to upload a logo
- no output (Word bid pack, case study, slides, PDF, Markdown) draws one
- the flag is stored and shown as a badge, but no code reads it to render
  anything

The user asked "how can we add logo here?" on 2026-10-14. A referenceable
client whose logo may be used is a strong sales asset, and it's missing
from every document we produce.

## Proposed outcome

- **Upload in the registry.** On each client's Edit panel, an admin can
  upload a logo (PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MB), see a preview, replace it, or
  remove it.
  - The file is re-encoded on the server, so no SVG or script can get
    through.
  - It is stored with the client.
- **Shown only when both flags allow it.** A logo appears in outputs only
  when the client is **referenceable** and **Logo allowed** is ticked. If
  either flag is off, it never appears, even if a file is stored.
  - Unticking either flag removes it from the next output at once.
- **Where it shows:**
  - the case-study cover
  - the bid pack case heading
  - the reference slide
  - next to the client name on the case's preview in the portal
- **Audit.** Logo uploads and removals are recorded, like other registry
  changes.

## Affected users and systems

- Admins (the registry) and everyone who generates outputs.
- Code:
  - `app/clients.py` and `clients.html` (upload, preview, remove)
  - storage, the same pattern as case diagrams (`app/diagrams.py`
    `_decode`)
  - `app/render.py` (docx, case study, pptx, PDF, Markdown alt text)
  - the preview template
- The schema gets a logo column or table.

## Constraints

- **Never shown for anonymised clients.** A protected client's logo would
  reveal who they are.
- **Safe images only.** Raster images, size-capped, decoded and
  re-encoded server-side.
- **Admins only** can upload. The logo is served with the same image
  headers as diagrams (`PNG_HEADERS`).
- **Customer templates keep their layout.** A missing logo leaves no gap.

## Open questions

1. **Where in each output?** Proposed:
   - case-study cover: top-right, 40 mm wide at most
   - bid pack: beside the case title, 25 mm at most
   - slide: top-right corner
2. **Should the portal's search cards also show the logo** for
   referenceable clients? Proposed: yes, small (24 px) in place of the
   building icon. Search is internal, and the same flags apply.
