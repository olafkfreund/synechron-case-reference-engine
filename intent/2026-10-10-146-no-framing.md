# Intent: Portal pages can be framed (no X-Frame-Options / frame-ancestors)

## Problem

No response sets `X-Frame-Options` or a CSP `frame-ancestors`. That is true
of the `guard` middleware (`app/main.py:105-118`), the templates and the ALB
(`infra/alb.tf`).

Another site on the same parent domain can load `/review/<id>` in an
invisible frame. The guard's own comment expects sibling subdomains. In that
case:

- the `SameSite=lax` session cookie is sent, because the request is
  same-site;
- a click inside the frame posts from the portal's own origin, so the
  origin/CSRF check passes.

So a reviewer can be tricked into approving a case they never read with one
click, and it is then usable in bids for 12 months.

## Proposed outcome

No portal page can be shown inside a frame on another page. Browsers refuse to
render it there.

## Affected users and systems

- Every page, via `app/main.py` (the `guard` middleware).
- Tests: `tests/test_hardening.py` or `tests/test_auth.py`.

## Constraints

- Server-rendered, with no JavaScript, so there is no frame-busting script.
  Use headers only.
- No page legitimately frames the portal.

## Open questions

None. `guard` adds `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and
`Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'` to every response.
