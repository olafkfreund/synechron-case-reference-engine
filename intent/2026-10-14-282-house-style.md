# Intent: outputs in the Synechron house style

## Problem

Every document the engine produces still uses the placeholder templates in
`brand/`. The Word, PowerPoint and PDF outputs carry a red "DRAFT TEMPLATE"
header and default Word styling, and use IBM Plex. Architecture diagrams use
their own off-brand colours. Nothing a bid team sends out looks like
Synechron.

The brand now exists in two skills:

- **`synechron-pptx-v2`, the authoritative rules:**
  - Aptos
  - seven colours: #002535, #0A5A78, #FBF321, #FFFFFF, #000000, #808285
    and #F4F4F4
  - logo clear space and colourways
  - cover options, including a mandatory yellow closing cover
  - rounded containers
  - the 60/40 gradient
- **`synechron-pptx`:** the official June 2026 Light and Dark `.potx`
  templates, with a vector wordmark.

There is no official Word or PDF template.

## Proposed outcome

1. **PowerPoint.** Generated decks use the official Light template:
   - an opening cover
   - one branded slide per reference case, plus the industry context and
     diagram slides
   - the mandatory yellow closing cover
   - the Synechron wordmark, footer and page numbers from the master
2. **Word.** The reference, case study and brief documents are rebuilt in
   the brand:
   - Aptos headings and body
   - the approved palette, with yellow only as an accent
   - the vector wordmark with its clear space
   - a branded first page for the case study
   - a footer with page numbers
3. **PDF.** PDFs, converted from either format, render in Aptos and look
   the same as the Office files.
4. **Diagrams.** Architecture diagrams use the palette, Aptos and rounded
   boxes.
5. **Two companion skills,** `synechron-docx` and `synechron-pdf`, next
   to `synechron-pptx-v2` in the skills folder. They carry the same
   brand rules for Word and PDF, so people and agents outside the engine
   build documents the same way.

**Decided with the requester (2026-10-14):**

- Aptos in all formats, PDF included, installed from the Microsoft download at build time (not committed: EULA)
- the Light template
- the official template and wordmark committed in `brand/`

## Affected users and systems

- `brand/`: `master.pptx`, `reference.docx`, `case_study.docx`,
  `brief.docx`, `fonts/`
- `scripts/make_reference_template.py`
- `app/render.py`: `to_pptx` covers, the font of the technical notes
- `app/archdiagram.py`: palette and font
- `Dockerfile`: fonts
- the tests that pin the template contract
- the skills folder outside this repo
- everyone who downloads outputs

## Constraints

- **The named-layout contract stays:** the "Reference case", "Industry
  context" and "Diagram" layouts and their placeholder names. The master
  is adapted to it, not the code to the master.
- **Client logos and anonymisation don't change.** A client logo still
  appears only when the client is referenceable, permissioned and not
  masked. The Synechron wordmark is fixed artwork, separate from client
  logos.
- **Only the official vector artwork** from the `.potx` is used. The
  low-resolution v2 reference JPGs are never used in outputs, and their
  measurement marks are never reproduced.
- **The v2 rules win** wherever v1 differs, for example v1's extra palette
  colours.
- **The committed master is stripped** of sample slides and unused media,
  to keep the repo small.

- **Aptos comes from Microsoft's official download.** Decided with the
  requester. Source: "Microsoft Aptos Fonts" 4.40,
  `https://download.microsoft.com/download/8/6/0/860a94fa-7feb-44ef-ac79-c072d9113d69/Microsoft%20Aptos%20Fonts.zip`,
  SHA-256 `6528fd120e719a9f985e94214eca6887d1653b88456916a792a630b02e95b025`.
  - **Installed by the Docker build, not committed.** The build downloads
    the zip, checks the pinned hash, and installs the fonts in the image.
  - **Why not committed:** the EULA grants "install and use any number of
    copies … on your devices", but "No Distribution Rights", and forbids
    "publish the software for others to copy". Font files in git would be
    copies others can take. Installing them in our own image, on our own
    servers, is within the licence.
  - **Embedding is allowed:** the fonts' fsType is `0x8` (editable
    embedding), so PDFs and Office files may embed Aptos.
  - **One term to note:** the EULA forbids use "for commercial software
    hosting services". This is an internal tool for Synechron staff, not a
    service hosted for others.
