# Intent: research says why sources were skipped

## Problem

The research page shows only a count of skipped sources: "8 source(s)
skipped". When every source is skipped, as for an SAP question where every
SAP site disallows crawlers, the page says "No usable sources found" and
gives no reason. The user can't tell whether to rephrase, try again later,
or look elsewhere.

The worker already records why each source was skipped
(`app/research.py:840`), but the page throws that away.

## Proposed outcome

Under Sources, skipped sources are grouped by reason in plain words, for
example "8 skipped: 6 don't allow automated access, 2 didn't respond in
time". The reasons:

- don't allow automated access
- didn't respond in time
- couldn't be reached
- too large
- not a web page or PDF
- refused by the server
- not a public address

Sources hidden to protect a client stay in the count with no reason, as
today.

## Affected users and systems

- `app/research.py` (`research_view`)
- `app/templates/research_view.html`
- research users

## Constraints

- **No domain or URL of a skipped source is shown.** It could name a
  client.
- **Hidden sources give nothing away:** no reason is shown for them, only
  the count, as now.
- **No new data is stored.** Older research rows are read as they are.
