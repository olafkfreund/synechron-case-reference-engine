# Plan: A protected name written with a dot the registry doesn't have leaks into downloads

## The problem

With "St James's Bank" registered, the text "St. James's Bank" gets past
`apply()`, `blocked()` and `scrub()`. The name then leaks into downloads,
search summaries and research queries.

## Approved decisions (from the spec)

- A dot after any word of a registered name is optional when matching. The
  joiner between words accepts an optional dot (`\.?`) before the usual
  separators (`_JOIN`).
- **The last word gets no new dot.** "Zorp Co." already matches "Zorp Co"
  through the `(?!\w)` lookaround, and the dot stays as text ("a firm. They").
- **Inner dots in initials are not dropped**, which is the #163 rule:
  - "US Bank" doesn't match "U.S. Bank".
  - Registry "U.S. Bank" doesn't match "us bank".
  - `_loose` is unchanged.
- Hiding a sentence break such as "Zorp. Co-founders" is accepted.
- Names with no word character keep the plain joiner (`loose is str`).
