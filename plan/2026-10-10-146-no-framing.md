# Plan: No portal page can be framed by another page

Approved decisions (from the spec):
- Every response carries `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and
  `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'`. Both are sent: the CSP for
  current browsers, XFO for older ones. The CSP holds only `frame-ancestors`.
- The headers are set by the app in `guard`, the outermost middleware, so routes,
  `/healthz`, static files, the 401 redirect and guard's own 403/413 all get them.
  Not set on the ALB; no full CSP; no frame-busting script; no `SameSite=strict`.
- Known gap, accepted: Starlette's `ServerErrorMiddleware` plain 500 bypasses guard.

Two files, two file-editing steps: below the coder threshold, the session implements it.
