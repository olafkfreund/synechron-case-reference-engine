  `-500` and `-600` and `ibm-plex-mono-400` as `.woff2` (latin), from
  `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans@5/files/ibm-plex-sans-latin-<w>-normal.woff2`
  and `.../@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono@5/files/ibm-plex-mono-latin-400-normal.woff2`,
  plus `OFL.txt` (the SIL Open Font License 1.1 text from the same
  package, `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans@5/LICENSE`). `@font-face` uses
  `font-display: swap`, falling back to `system-ui`.
- **Tokens:**
  - ink `#1B2430`, ground `#F6F5F1`, accent `#0B6A6A` (hover `#084F4F`);
  - cards white with a `#DAD7CF` border and 10px radius;
  - muted `#55606E`, line `#ECE9E2`, table header `#F1EFEA`;
  - badges: ok `#E5F2E9`/`#1E6B3A`, warn `#FBF0D9`/`#6B4600`, bad
    `#F3E3E1`/`#8A2A1E`, neutral `#EEF1F4`/`#3D4652`.
- **Classes:** `.card`, `.badge` (+`.ok` `.warn` `.bad` `.neutral`), `.btn`
  (+`.primary` `.dark`, at least 44px), `.note`, `.tag`, `.mono`,
  `.table-box`, `.row`, `.stack`, `.muted`. The existing `.bad`, `.ok`,
  `.empty`, `.badge` and `.note` names keep working.
- **Header:**
  - navy bar: "Reference Engine", mono "bids & presales", the nav links,
    then initials, name, role and Log out;
  - links: Find references, Research; Review queue for reviewers; Sources,
    Client registry, Model approvals and Audit for admins;
  - `aria-current="page"` from `request.url.path` (prefix match);
  - a skip link to `<main>`; `<main>` max 1200px.
- **No mockup "sample data" banner.**
- **Coder handoff:** 7 file-editing steps, so steps 1–7 go to the `coder`
  agent. Step 8 (the reload of the live demo, screenshots) is the session
  model's.
