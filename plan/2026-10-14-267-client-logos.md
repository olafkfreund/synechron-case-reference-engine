# Plan: Client logos in the registry and in outputs

Worktree `../refs-engine-267`, branch `feat/267-client-logos`. One PR.

## Approved decisions (from the spec)

1. **Storage.** A new table `client_logos`: one row per client, holding
   `raw`, then `png`, `width`, `height` and `reason`. `clients` and its
   `VERSION` are unchanged.
2. **Upload.**
   - Admin only. PNG or JPEG by magic bytes, at most `LOGO_MAX_BYTES` (1 MiB).
   - A `logo` job is queued, and the worker decodes with
     `diagrams._decode()`.
   - Rejected: under 64 px on the short side, or an aspect ratio beyond
     8:1. `raw` is cleared after decoding.
   - Remove deletes the row.
3. **The display rule.** `logo_for(conn, client_id)` returns a PNG only
   if the client is referenceable **and** logo-allowed **and** the logo is
   decoded. Every surface uses it, and a protected client never gets one.
4. **Routes.**
   - `GET /admin/clients/{cid}/logo.png`, for admins.
   - `GET /logos/{cid}.png`, for any user. It gives 404 unless decision 3
     holds.
   - Both use `PNG_HEADERS`.
5. **Placement:**
   - bid pack docx: beside the title, 25 mm at most
   - case study: cover top-right, 40 mm at most
   - pptx: top-right, 25 mm high at most
   - Markdown: none
   - preview and search cards: 24 px, in place of the building icon
6. **Templates** come from `scripts/make_reference_template.py`, with
   conditional `c.logo` fields, regenerated rather than hand-edited.
7. **Audit.** An append-only `client_logo_changes` table, following
   `architecture_changes` (`sql/schema.sql:301`, `sql/roles.sql:24`), shown
   in `/admin/audit`.

## Steps

1. **Schema and worker.**
   - `sql/schema.sql`: `client_logos` and `client_logo_changes`.
   - `sql/roles.sql`: revoke update and delete on `client_logo_changes`.
   - `app/diagrams.py`: `process_logo(client_id)`, following `process`
     (`:51`), plus the size and aspect checks.
   - `app/worker.py:17`: a `"logo"` handler.
   - → verify: worker tests. A PNG and a JPEG succeed; a bomb, a tiny
     image, an extreme aspect ratio and garbage are rejected with a
     reason. `tests/test_db.py` includes the tables, and the
     append-only grant is tested.
2. **Routes and the rule** (`app/clients.py`).
   - Upload and remove, with the audit rows.
   - The admin preview route.
   - The public `/logos/{cid}.png`.
   - `logo_for()`.
   - Guard: `app/main.py:200` gets `LOGO_MAX_BYTES` for
     `/admin/clients/\d+/logo`.
   - → verify:
     - refusals by type, size and role
     - a `logo_for` truth table (referenceable × allowed × decoded)
     - the public route gives 404 unless the rule holds
     - audit rows are written
3. **Registry UI** (`app/templates/clients.html`). Inside the Edit panel:
   the preview, the status, an upload form and Remove. A "Logo ready"
   badge. Existing classes and tokens only.
   - → verify: `tests/test_ui.py` (colour lint, forms baseline updated
     only for the new form), and a page test.
4. **Outputs.**
   - `scripts/make_reference_template.py`: conditional logo fields in the
     reference, case-study and slide master, then regenerate `brand/`.
   - `app/render.py`: pass `logo` (`InlineImage`, sized as in decision 5)
     per section from `logo_for`, after `protect()`. Pictures go on the
     pptx slide.
   - Preview and search cards: `<img src="/logos/{cid}.png">` when allowed.
   - → verify:
     - an allowed client: the docx, case study and pptx contain the image
     - a client without permission or without a logo: no image, no
       "{{" text and no empty paragraph
     - a protected client: no logo in any format
   - Traps:
     - The template script is also edited by #213 and #231(c). Rebase onto
       whichever merged, then regenerate all templates from the merged
       script.
     - Never hand-edit `brand/*.docx`.
5. **Full suite, screenshots, reviews and PR.** The session takes light
   and dark screenshots of the registry with a logo and of a search card,
   then the Opus, Codex and agy reviews, then the PR:
   `docker compose build app && docker compose run --rm app pytest`.
