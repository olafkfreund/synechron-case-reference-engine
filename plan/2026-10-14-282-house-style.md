# Plan: outputs in the Synechron house style

## Approved decisions, carried over from the spec

- **Rules:** `synechron-pptx-v2/SKILL.md` is authoritative. Palette only:
  `#002535`, `#0A5A78`, `#FBF321` (accent only), `#FFFFFF`, `#000000`,
  `#808285`, `#F4F4F4`. Font: Aptos.
- **Artwork:** the official June 2026 templates in
  `~/Source/Calitti/synechron-skills/skills-main/skills/synechron-pptx/assets/`
  (`SK` below). The v2 reference JPGs are never used in outputs.
- **Aptos:**
  - Installed by the Docker build from
    `https://download.microsoft.com/download/8/6/0/860a94fa-7feb-44ef-ac79-c072d9113d69/Microsoft%20Aptos%20Fonts.zip`,
    SHA-256 `6528fd120e719a9f985e94214eca6887d1653b88456916a792a630b02e95b025`.
  - Installed to `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/aptos/`. Never committed
    (EULA: no distribution).
  - Embedding is allowed (fsType `0x8`).
- **Decks:** Light content slides, a blue Cover first and a blue Closing
  last. Both use cover option 2: `#002535` background, yellow device, white
  wordmark. The Light grey covers are not used.
- **Contract kept:** the layouts "Reference case", "Industry context" and
  "Diagram", with their placeholder names (`app/render.py:29-30`,
  `:363-368`).
- **Word:** the docxtpl tags and contexts are unchanged; only the styling
  changes. Callouts are shaded and borderless (no rounded Word tables:
  accepted exception).
- **Unchanged:** client logos and anonymisation (`render.build`,
  `protect`, `clients.logo_for`).
- **Removed:** IBM Plex (`brand/fonts/`). `Consolas` becomes `Aptos Mono`.
- **Companion skills:** `synechron-docx` and `synechron-pdf` go in the
  skills folder, outside this repo.

## Steps

worktree off `feat/282-house-style`, and touch disjoint files. Step D runs
alongside them, outside the repo. Step E integrates them.

**0. Me: fonts in the image and the wordmark assets.** Commit this before
the fan-out.

- **`Dockerfile`**, after the apt step: one `RUN` with
  `python -c` that does `urllib.request.urlretrieve` of the zip and checks
  `hashlib.sha256` (`sys.exit` on a mismatch). It extracts `Aptos.ttf`,
  `Aptos-Light.ttf`, `Aptos-SemiBold.ttf`, `Aptos-Bold.ttf`,
  `Aptos-Italic.ttf` and `Aptos-Mono.ttf` to
  `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/aptos/`, deletes the zip, and runs `fc-cache
  -f`. Add `fontconfig` to the apt list if `fc-cache` is missing.
- **`brand/wordmark-white.svg`, `brand/wordmark-black.svg` and
  `-{white,black}.png`:** the white and black wordmark SVGs from
  `SK/Synechron_Light_Presentation_Template_June_2026.potx`
  (`ppt/media/image8.svg` and `image9.svg`; confirm which is which by
  rendering). The PNGs are rasterised once with LibreOffice or ImageMagick
  at 2000 px wide, with a transparent background.
- **Check:** `docker compose build app && docker compose run --rm app
  fc-list | grep -c Aptos`, where the count must be at least 6.
- **Traps:**
  - The guard blocks `$(...)` in double-quoted commit messages; use
    `-F -`.
  - Read the zip in a scratch directory, never inside the repo.
