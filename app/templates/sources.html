Sources

Data class: confidential (the default) documents are only sent to local or own-cloud models unless approved; mark a source sanitised or public only when that is true.

Contracts executed: tick it only when every statement of work or change order in the source is signed; each then becomes an engagement reference without the signature check.

Access groups: the Entra groups whose members can read this source. Every document from it gets these groups. SharePoint files whose permissions differ from the site root, and Confluence pages with a read restriction on themselves or an ancestor (or under a folder), are skipped. The crawler cannot see who may read a whole site or space: enter only groups whose members can read all of it. The Confluence crawler account must be a dedicated non-admin account that no restriction names.
