Sources

Where reference documents are crawled from, and who may read them.

Data classes

Data class: confidential (the default) documents are only sent to local or own-cloud models unless approved; mark a source sanitised or public only when that is true.

Contracts executed

Tick it only when every statement of work or change order in the source is signed; each then becomes an engagement reference without the signature check. Ticking it also queues contracts already crawled that have no case; unticking it retires the engagements it created, approved ones included.

Access groups

The Entra groups whose members can read this source. Every document from it gets these groups. SharePoint files whose permissions differ from the site root, and Confluence pages with a read restriction on themselves or an ancestor (or under a folder), are skipped. The crawler cannot see who may read a whole site or space: enter only groups whose members can read all of it. The Confluence crawler account must be a dedicated non-admin account that no restriction names.
